# Vanu

> Vanu, Inc. builds wireless network solutions that extend cellular coverage to areas that cannot be profitably served with conventional technology — rural regions, buildings, tunnels, and maritime environments. Founded in 1998 out of MIT software-radio research and headquartered in Lexington, MA (offices in Gurgaon, Bangalore, and Kigali), Vanu ships the Anywave dual-mode GSM/LTE base station (the first FCC-certified software-defined radio), Community Connect village systems, portable LTE networks, coverage mapping (VanuMaps), and coverage-as-a-service for mobile carriers. Vanu publishes no public developer API surface; this file was generated by API Evangelist from catalog research (/.well-known/* and /llms.txt probes all returned 404 on 2026-07-21).
